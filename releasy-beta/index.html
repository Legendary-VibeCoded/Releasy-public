If Windows or Chrome warns you

The beta installer is not signed with a paid certificate yet, so Chrome may say "Dangerous download blocked" and Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". That is expected for a new, unsigned app.

  1. In Chrome, open the downloads list and choose Keep.
  2. In SmartScreen, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Only do this for the file you downloaded from this page.
Your mission

Make a complete package for one real track.

Have a WAV or MP3 of your own track ready, plus its cover art (JPG or PNG). Then work through the app the way you would for a real release.

1. Release and snippets

Add your track and cover art, fill in title, artist and date. Pick snippets on the waveform, or press Suggest loudest part.

2. Look and text

Try a few looks, shuffle colors, change the background and visualizer. Set the top line, bottom line, badge and a text animation.

3. Formats

Go through every format in the preview. Turn on Safe zones and check that nothing important is hidden.

4. Lyrics and stickers

Optional but valuable: paste lyrics, stamp the timing, fix a line on purpose. Add a sticker and move, resize and rotate it.

5. Release Planner

Check the plan, captions and hashtags. Save the schedule as a .ics calendar file and as a .csv.

6. Presets and export

Save a preset, reload the app and load it again. Then export several snippets and formats at once, and open the files.

Good to know

Known limitations.

Files are not kept

Your track, cover art and backgrounds are not remembered after you close the app. Titles, looks, snippets and the plan are. Presets keep most files, but never audio or video.

Safe zones are approximate

Platforms change their interfaces, so leave some room around anything important.

Desktop screens only

Phones are not supported. Calendar export is a .ics file you import yourself.

Press ? for help

The ? button next to Settings (or F1) opens the full manual inside the app.

What to look out for

  • Did the export finish? How long did it take, and on what computer?
  • Does the exported video match the preview? Is the audio in sync?
  • Are audio playback and lyric stamping accurate?
  • Where did you get stuck or confused?
  • What did you expect to find but could not?

How to report

  1. Open Settings > Beta > Copy version info and paste it into your message.
  2. Tell me what you did, step by step.
  3. Say what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps.
  4. Add your computer (for example "Windows 11").
