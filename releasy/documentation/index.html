Releasy turns a track into a complete social media package for a release: teaser videos in every format, captions, hashtags and a release schedule. You make the look once and export everything in one go.

This is the same text as the built-in Help in Releasy v1.1.5. In the app, press F1 or the ? button to open it.

The big picture

How the app is organised

  • Header: the project name (click it to rename the title and artist), the theme buttons, Presets, Settings, the ? Help button (or press F1), Undo and Redo, and Export package.
  • Three views (top left): Editor, Lyrics and Release Planner.
  • Editor has a side panel with four tabs (Release, Look, Text, Stickers) and, on the right, the preview, the player, and the snippet strip with its waveform.
  • Search bar (bottom of the screen, press /): type what you want to change ("blur", "margins", "tags") and jump straight to that setting.
    • What does this do? On the highlighted search result a ? appears. Click it, or hold Enter (or Space on an empty search) for half a second, to see a short explanation of that setting. A quick press of Enter still jumps to it. Press Esc to close the explanation.
    • Most settings have an explanation. Self-explanatory ones (such as Title) have no ?.

The three ideas to understand

  1. Snippet: a part of your song (3 seconds or longer) that one teaser shows. A project holds up to 6 snippets. Each snippet has its own start and end, name, top line, bottom line and status badge.
  2. Look: colours, fonts, background, cover art style, visualizer and so on. By default every snippet shares one look, so changing it changes them all. Turn on Unique look for this snippet if one snippet should differ.
  3. Format: the video type (Reels, TikTok, YouTube and so on). The preview shows one format at a time, and some settings (margins, which elements are shown, cover art size) are remembered per format.

Little things worth knowing

  • The Settings and Presets windows open with a brief glowing edge, the same effect as the export warning, so they are easy to spot.
  • Search finds the theme list when you type words like "theme", "dark", "random" or "tint".

What stays on your computer

Everything. Your audio, pictures and videos are never uploaded and videos are rendered on your computer. Your project settings are saved automatically inside the app, on this computer. See Saving your work below.

A quick workflow

  1. Release tab: add your track, add cover art, fill in title, artist and release date.
  2. Snippet strip: drag on the waveform to choose the part of the song to show, or press Suggest loudest part. Add more with + New snippet.
  3. Look tab: pick a look and press Shuffle colors until it feels right. Adjust background, visualizer and cover art.
  4. Text tab: set the top line, bottom line, status badge, fonts and text animation.
  5. Switch Format above the preview to check every video type.
  6. Optional: Lyrics view for timed lyrics, Stickers tab for stickers, Release Planner for captions and a schedule.
  7. Export package (top right).

Release tab

Track

  • Add your track: a WAV or MP3. It stays on your computer. Until you add one, a demo signal plays so you can still design.
  • Reaction track (optional): for example drums only. The visualizers, cover art pulse and moving backgrounds follow this track instead of the full mix, but you still hear your main track. It should start at the same point and have the same length as the main track. Remove reaction track undoes it.

Cover art

  • Add cover art: the colours for your looks are taken from it. It also appears in the video.
  • Until you add cover art, a set of sample colours is shown. These change each time the app opens, picking from a few colour families.

Release

  • Title and Artist(s): shown in the video, captions and file names.
  • Release date: feeds the status badge and the Release Planner. Leave it blank for no date. Date fields have their own calendar popup; the order and language follow Settings → Language and dates.

Look tab

The Look tab is a stack of collapsible blocks. By default, opening one closes the others (change this in Settings → Interface → Option blocks).

Synced look

  • Unique look for this snippet: Off: all snippets share one look. On: this snippet keeps its own look. Turning it off again brings back the shared look.
  • Margins are set per format, never per snippet.

Looks and colors

  • Looks are ready-made styles: Night bars, Vinyl ring, Cover glow, Dot matrix, Pastel, Rings, Type only, Poster, Aurora, Tidal, Plasma, Warp, Halftone, Ridgeline, Sunburst and Terminal. Each sets fonts, background, visualizer, cover art style and text position as a starting point. Your own settings (badge, media, fades, filters and so on) are kept when you switch.
  • Shuffle colors: re-colours the look using your cover art colours. Press it repeatedly until you like the result.
  • Use this look on all snippets, Copy look, Paste look: move a look between snippets.
  • Colors: the colour swatches the look uses. Click one to change it.

Style, movement and size (cover art)

  • Cover art style: Card, Spinning disc, Polaroid, Glow frame, Tilted card, Vinyl and sleeve, Reflection, Gallery frame, or Hidden.
  • Cover art movement: how the art moves.
  • Size per video type: one size per format, shared by all snippets. Moving a slider switches the preview to that format. Reset all to 100% resets them.
  • Cover art pulses with the music and Spinning disc keeps turning: master switches for this snippet.
  • Audio reaction sensitivity (0–200%): scales every audio-reactive movement (cover art, visualizers, backgrounds). 0% is static, 100% is default, 200% is exaggerated. Applies to all snippets and exports.
  • Per video type: choose "Doesn't move" for a format to keep the cover still in the preview and its export.

Background

  • Background types: Gradient, Soft blobs, Aurora, Waves, Plasma, Starfield warp, Halftone pulse, Retro grid, Light rays, Bokeh, Ripples, Stage beams, Your image, video or GIF, Solid, Green screen (for keying), Blurred cover, Cover full-bleed.
  • Pattern over the background: None, Dots, Lines, Grid, Diagonal stripes, Rings. Pattern details (size/spacing, thickness, opacity, speed, angle, centre height, dot shape, dashed lines, colour) and Reset pattern details.
  • Background texture: None, Film grain, Scanlines, Grain and scanlines, Paper, Dust and hairs, Old film, VHS, Crosshatch, Dark vignette, Light leaks.
  • Background movement and Background blur.
  • Movement amount for background and cover art.

Background media

Use your own image, video or GIF as the background.

  • Add image, video or GIF, then choose Use as background. Your file stays on this computer and is not kept when you close the app or reload the page. Saving a preset keeps images and GIFs; a background video has to be added again.
  • Fit: Fill the frame or Fit inside. Dim darkens it so text stays readable.
  • Start the video at a chosen second. Videos and GIFs loop and restart with each snippet.
  • Scale, Move left/right, Move up/down to position it.
  • Color filter: hue, saturation, lightness, contrast, with Reset adjustments.
  • Audio mix, video sound: 0% is the song only, 100% is only the video's own sound. Change over time (keyframes): click to add a point, drag to move it, double-click a point to remove it. Top of the graph is video sound only, bottom is the song only. Add keyframe at playhead adds one at the current time.
  • Remove media clears it.

Visualizer

  • Types: Bars, Mirrored bars, Line, Dot matrix, Ring, LED meter, Waveform, Mountain, Ridge lines, Sunburst, Pulse rings, Orbs, Columns, or None.
  • Position: Bottom, Center or Top. Size as a percentage.
  • You can click the visualizer in the preview to select it, like the other elements.

Margins

Push part of the video away from an edge, in pixels. "Everything" moves the cover art, visualizer and all text together. Text margins are on the Text tab; lyrics have their own margin here. Margins are saved per format and shared by all snippets. The heading shows which format you are editing. Reset margins clears them.

Fades

  • Fade video in and out (through black) and Fade sound in and out, with an adjustable fade length. Applies to this snippet and to exports. Spotify Canvas loops are never faded.

Show on the video

Switch elements on or off: Cover art, Background (image, media, effects), Visualizer, Stickers. Switching one off hides it without losing its settings. These switches apply to every snippet but only for the format selected above the preview: each format keeps its own. Text elements have their own switches on the Text tab.

Text tab

Text strings

  • Title and Artist(s) (also editable on the Release tab).
  • Top line (above the title) and Bottom line (below the artist): short hook lines. These are per snippet, so each teaser can say something different.

Status badge

A small pill above the top line.

  • Badge shows: Nothing, Release date, Coming soon, Out now, or Custom text.
  • Date style, Badge style and Badge position (between art and text, above the cover art, or below the text).
  • The release date is shared with the Release tab and the Release Planner. If no date is set, the badge stays empty.

Overall text style

  • Look font and case: the default for every text.
  • Position: Top, Center, Bottom. Alignment: Auto, Left, Center, Right.
  • Title effect: None, Glow, Outline.
  • Overall size: multiplies every text size.

Size and font per text

Open a text to change its font, size, spacing, style, colour and margins. "All text" holds the default font, case and one margin that moves every text together. Sizes multiply the overall size. Choose Upload a font file… in any font list to use your own TTF, OTF or WOFF for that text only. Clear per-text custom styles removes your individual overrides.

Text animation

How the text appears.

  • Effects: Rise and fade in, Drop and fade in, Slide in from the left, Slide in from the right, Fade in, Zoom in, Zoom out, Pop (overshoot), Blur in, Spread out (letter spacing), Flip up, Rotate in, Type the title, Word by word, Wipe, or None.
  • Settings that appear depend on the effect: duration, start delay, delay between lines, distance, start size, blur amount, spread, angle, wipe direction, easing (Smooth, Soft, Snappy, Overshoot, Linear) and a typing cursor.
  • Replay plays the animation again in the preview. Reset animation returns it to the defaults.

Margins

Push a text away from an edge, in pixels. "All text" moves everything together. Saved per format and shared by all snippets. Reset text margins clears them.

Show on the video

Per format (same idea as on the Look tab): Title and artist, Status badge, Top line, Bottom line, Hashtags, Lyrics.

  • Hashtags: when switched on, a slider sets how many to show (1–15, up to three lines). Hashtags come from the Release Planner (genre, "always include" and "extra" tags).
  • Lyrics are set up in the Lyrics view.
  • By default the YouTube full-song format starts with the top/bottom lines and status badge turned off.

Stickers tab

  • Add stickers: PNG with transparency, JPG or animated GIF. You can also drag image files straight onto the preview.
  • Ready-made stickers: click one to add it. The coloured ones follow your look's accent colour.
  • Move, resize, rotate: click a sticker in the preview to select it, then drag it. Corner handles resize; the round handle rotates (hold Shift to snap to 15°). Arrow keys nudge by 1 pixel, with Shift by 10.
  • Selected sticker panel: Size, Rotation, Opacity, exact Position (X%, Y%), Snap to a corner/edge/centre, Flip, Duplicate, Lock, Bring to front, Send to back, Delete.
  • Lock stops a sticker being moved or changed until you unlock it.
  • Show on: all snippets, or this snippet only. Show in formats: choose which formats show it.
  • Own position in this format: Off: the sticker sits in the same place in every format. On: moving it only affects this format. Use this position in all formats copies the position everywhere.

The preview, player and snippets

Preview

  • Format menu: YouTube full song (16:9), YouTube Shorts (9:16), Instagram Reels (9:16), Instagram feed (4:5), TikTok (9:16), Spotify Canvas (9:16). A note beside the menu says what that platform covers.
  • Hover the preview to show Safe zones (an overlay of areas platforms cover with their own buttons and captions; approximate), Zoom, and Reset. They fade away when the mouse leaves.
  • Click elements in the preview (cover art, title, visualizer, stickers) to select them. A soft highlight shows what can be selected.

Player

Play/pause (Space), jump to start, a seek bar, volume, and time display.

Snippet strip

  • Tabs for each snippet and + New snippet (up to 6).
  • Waveform: drag the handles or the range to choose what the snippet shows. A snippet is at least 3 seconds.
  • Snippet name, Start (s), End (s), and the resulting Length.
  • Suggest loudest part finds a strong section for you.
  • Delete removes the snippet.

Lyrics view

Open it from the Lyrics button at the top. Use Back to editor to return.

Getting lyrics in

  • Paste lyrics and Send to Lines panel: type or paste your lyrics, then send them to the line list.

Timing (stamping)

  1. Play the song.
  2. Press Enter (or S, or the Stamp the next line button) whenever a line starts. The next empty time is filled in.
  3. Skip line marks a line [SKIP] so it never shows, useful for labels like [Chorus]. You can also type skip in a time box.
  4. Stamp empty line adds a blank line, used to make the lyrics disappear where the singing stops.
  5. Fixing a mistake: click a line in the list, then press Stamp the selected line while the song plays. That line gets the current time, even if it already had one.
  6. In a time box, the arrow keys nudge by 0.1 s (hold Shift for 1 s).
  7. Reaction delay compensates for your reaction time when stamping. Move all lyrics later or earlier shifts every time at once.
  8. Clear all times removes timing but keeps the text. Remove all lines clears everything. Save as .lrc file exports standard timed lyrics.

Style

  • Master font, Size, Line spacing, Dimmed lines (opacity of lines around the current one) and how many lines are shown around the current one (none, 1, 2 or 3 above and below).
  • Alignment (horizontal and vertical), Color, Capitals, Heavy weight.
  • Each line appears with, The lyrics leave with, A line moves away with: choose an effect and duration. Entrances include Hard cut, Fade, Slide up/down/left/right, Pop in, Zoom out to size, Blur in and Letter by letter; exits and move-aways have matching versions.
  • The ⋯ button on a line gives that line its own font, entrance, move-away and exit effects, or turns the looks off for it.
  • Long lines shrink to fit; split very long lines in two.
  • Reset style and Lyrics margins….

Looks (stack as many as you like)

Add visual effects to the lyrics. Available: Glow, Outline, Drop shadow, Gradient fill, Scanlines, Red-blue shift, Noise, Distortion and Flicker, each with its own settings. Reset looks clears them.

Preview

Choose Lyrics only (larger, just the lyrics) or Full video (over the whole picture in 16:9). Press play to watch the lyrics follow the song.

Showing lyrics in the video

Switch on Show lyrics on the video (here, or on the Text tab per format). Lyrics sit at the bottom. They are not shown on Spotify Canvas loops.

Release Planner

Release

  • Release date, Genre (for hashtags) and Link text (for example "link in bio").

Post copy

  • Always include (tags every post should have) and Extra hashtags.
  • Write captions and Add hashtags to post copy switch those parts on or off.

The plan

A ready-made rollout is created from your release date: announce and first teaser (3 weeks before), second teaser, cover reveal and pre-save, third teaser, three days to go, out tomorrow, release day, thank-you, one week out and a "behind the track" post two weeks after. Each post has a date, time, platforms, a type and caption text you can edit.

  • Add a post adds your own. Reset plan from template restores the original.
  • Save schedule (.ics): a calendar file you can import into any calendar app (Google, Apple, Outlook and others). Releasy does not connect to any calendar itself.
  • Save schedule (.csv) (a spreadsheet) and Save post copy (.txt) (all captions).

Export package

Press Export package. Before rendering, a check lists anything not set yet (for example no audio or no release date) and lets you go back or continue anyway.

Choose what to export

  • Snippets: which snippets to render.
  • Formats: which video types to render. YouTube full song and Spotify Canvas are made once (not per snippet).
  • On the video: which elements to show in the exported videos (title/artist, status badge, top line, bottom line, hashtags, lyrics).
  • Also export without the status badge: every video that shows the coming soon / release date badge is rendered twice, with and without. The second file ends in -no-badge, handy for after release.
  • File names: a pattern with {title}, {artist}, {snippet}, {format}, {date}. The first file name is shown as an example.
  • Post copy and schedule: choose captions, hashtags in post copy, and schedule files (.ics, .csv, post-copy.txt).

Rendering and saving

  • Render package starts. Progress shows per video; Cancel stops it.
  • Save into a folder, as a .zip, or file by file.
  • Videos are always exported at full quality. The preview quality setting only affects the editor.
  • MP4 files are written directly on your computer.

Presets

Open from the header. A preset stores every control value, your snippets, text, release planner and theme, plus uploaded fonts, stickers, cover art and background image or GIF. Audio and video files are not included, so add your track again after loading.

  • Save the current setup: type a name and press Save. Presets are saved inside the app.
  • Download as a file and Load from a file: back up presets or move them to another computer.
  • Saved in the app: your list. Load or delete from here.
  • Preset storage can also be opened from Settings → Preset storage.

Settings

Open from the header, or search for a setting.

Interface

  • Font and Text size of the app itself.
  • Option blocks in the side panel: auto-close or keep open.
  • Search results: how many are shown in the search list.
  • Search: how many results the search list shows, and how long you hold Enter to see a setting's explanation (0.3 to 1.5 seconds, 0.5 by default).
  • Helpful tooltips: on or off. Replay the welcome tour.

Preview

Only changes the editor preview. Exports are always full quality.

  • Frame rate and Resolution (lighter settings help on slow computers).
  • At the end of the snippet: loop or play once.
  • When I switch to another window: keep playing or pause.
  • Play button: resume or always start from the beginning.
  • Lyrics list while the song plays: stay or scroll to the playing line.

Dates and files

  • Language for day and month names, Date order, Week starts on. These affect date fields, the plan, the status badge, captions and exports.
  • Name pattern for exported videos, with Use the default pattern.

Saved data

  • Reset all controls to default: keeps your files, text, snippets and stickers.
  • Reset app and clear cache: removes everything the app remembers, including saved presets. Your license and the trial clock are kept. You are asked to confirm and can restart now or later.

About and What's new

The About tab shows the version number, with a Copy version info button that copies the version and your browser details so a problem can be traced to the right version. It also has the credits, and a View the changelog button that lists what changed in each version.

Beta and feedback

Releasy is still in beta, so some errors can be expected. The Beta tab, the last tab in Settings, explains this and has the same View the changelog and Copy version info buttons. To report a bug or suggest an idea, press Open the feedback form (it opens in your browser), and paste the copied version info into it.

License and trial

A new install of Releasy is a free 7-day trial with every feature. The header shows how many days are left. When the trial ends, Releasy asks for a license key; your projects and presets are not deleted.

To activate, click the trial or license button in the header (or search for License), type the 25-character key from your purchase email and press Activate. Releasy needs the internet for this one step; after that it works offline. No key yet? The license window has a link to legendaryhq.net/releasy, which opens in your browser. A key is for one computer.

New computer or reinstall? Enter your key as usual. If it says the key is already activated on another computer, a Move license to this computer section appears. Type the email address you used for the purchase and press the button. Each key can be moved 3 times, and you get an email about every move. Please stop using Releasy on the old computer. If the email does not match or the moves are used up, email support@legendaryhq.net.

Colour themes

  • Dark, Light, OLED, Themed buttons in the header.
  • Themed opens a theme list with many colour themes, including Random on start (a new tint each time the app opens; pick it again to re-roll) and Custom theme…, where you set all ten interface colours yourself. Custom colours are saved with your project.

Saving your work

  • Your project settings (text, looks, snippets, plan) are saved automatically inside the app, on this computer as you work.
  • Files you load are not kept when you close the app or reload the page: your track, reaction track, cover art, background media, stickers and uploaded fonts all have to be added again. A preset keeps your cover art, uploaded fonts, stickers and a background image or GIF, so save one when you stop working. A background video and your audio are never included.
  • Undo / Redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y) reach back through your changes.
  • Moving to another computer or removing the app's data means starting over. Download presets as files for anything you would hate to lose.
  • If saving fails (storage full), a message explains the cause and what to do.

Privacy and offline use

  • No AI, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Colours, captions, hashtags, schedules and videos come from plain rules and your own input.
  • The only things the app sends over the internet are the license check (your key, a random computer ID and the app version, see the privacy policy) and the typefaces fetched from Google Fonts. Your music, pictures and videos are never sent. Without internet the app still works once your license is activated: built-in fonts fall back to system fonts, and your own uploaded fonts are unaffected. Choose System font as the interface font and upload font files for video text to avoid Google Fonts entirely.

Troubleshooting

  • No sound or no movement: check that a track is added and the volume is up. With no track, only the demo signal plays.
  • Preview is choppy: lower Settings → Preview → Frame rate or Resolution.
  • Text hidden: check the Show on the video switches for the selected format, and that the text is not set to the same colour as the background.
  • A format looks different from another: margins, shown elements and cover art size are per format by design.
  • My track, cover art or background is gone after reopening: files are not kept between sessions. Add them again, or load a preset that includes them.
  • Export is WebM, not MP4: this computer could not write MP4 files.
  • "Your draft couldn't be saved": storage is full or blocked. Remove big background media or old presets, use a normal (non-private) window, and save a preset.
  • "The saved draft was damaged": load a preset you saved earlier.
  • A font in the preset wouldn't load: add the font again under the Text tab's font upload, or choose another.

Good to know

  • Safe zones are approximate; platforms change their interfaces, so leave extra room around anything important.
