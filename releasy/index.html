What it does

Everything a release needs, in one window.

Stop rebuilding the same teaser in five different apps. Make the look once and let Releasy do the formats.

Teasers in every format

Switch between vertical, square and wide formats while you work. Safe zones show what each platform covers, and margins are remembered per format.

Snippets from your song

Drag on the waveform to pick your best seconds, or let Releasy suggest the loudest part. Keep up to six snippets, each with its own text and badge.

Looks that match your cover

Colors come from your cover art. Try backgrounds, visualizers, fonts and text animations, then shuffle until it feels like the record.

Lyrics, timed to the beat

Paste your lyrics, stamp each line while the song plays, fix any line by hand, then show them on the video in your own styles.

Release Planner

A day-by-day post schedule with captions and hashtags. Save it as a calendar file (.ics) or a spreadsheet (.csv).

Presets and one-click export

Save a whole look as a preset for the next release. Export every snippet and format at once as a folder or a .zip.

Before you install

Why Windows or your browser may call Releasy "unsafe".

You may see "Dangerous download blocked" in Chrome, or "Windows protected your PC" from SmartScreen. That sounds alarming, so here is what it actually means.

These warnings do not mean the app was scanned and found harmful. They appear for any installer that is new and not yet widely downloaded, or that is not signed with a paid code-signing certificate. Releasy is a small independent app, so it falls in both groups for now.

How to install it anyway

  1. Only if you got it from this page. Never run a copy from anywhere else.
  2. In Chrome: open the downloads list, find the Releasy installer and choose Keep.
  3. In Windows SmartScreen: click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Follow the installer. Releasy installs for your user account only and needs no admin rights.
What Releasy does not do: it does not upload your music, ask for an account or send your files anywhere. Your audio, pictures and videos stay on your computer. The only thing fetched online is the fonts.

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

What do I need to get started?

A WAV or MP3 of your track and, ideally, your cover art as a JPG or PNG. That is all. There is no account to create and no sign-in.

Which video formats can I export?

YouTube, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Instagram Feed, TikTok and Spotify Canvas. You can switch between them in the preview and check what each platform covers with the safe zones.

Does my music get uploaded anywhere?

No. Your audio, pictures and videos are never uploaded, and the videos are rendered on your own computer. The only thing fetched from the internet is the typefaces from Google Fonts.

Can I make more than one teaser from one song?

Yes. A project holds up to six snippets, each with its own start and end, name, top line, bottom line and status badge. Export them all, in all formats, in one go.

Can I show lyrics on the video?

Yes. Paste your lyrics, stamp each line to the music while the song plays, correct any timing by hand and show the lyrics in the style you like.

What do I get when I export?

Video files for each snippet and format, saved into a folder, as a .zip or file by file. You can also export a version without the status badge.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, once installed. Without a connection the built-in fonts fall back to system fonts, and fonts you upload yourself are unaffected.

Does Releasy remember my files between sessions?

Your titles, looks, snippets and plan are saved automatically. Files you load, such as your track, cover art and backgrounds, are not kept, so add them again next time. Presets keep most of them (not audio or video), so save one when you stop working.

Can I plan the release itself?

Yes. The Release Planner builds a post schedule with captions and hashtags. Save it as a .ics file for your calendar or a .csv for a spreadsheet.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. Windows comes first, and a Mac version is planned, with no date yet.

Is Releasy connected to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube or Spotify?

No. It is independent software. Those names only describe the video sizes and layouts Releasy can make.

System requirements

Windows 10 or 11. Releasy uses the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime, which is already part of up-to-date Windows 10 and 11. If it is missing, the installer downloads it for you, so you need an internet connection once.

Private by design

No accounts, no cloud rendering, no tracking. Your unreleased music never leaves your machine.
