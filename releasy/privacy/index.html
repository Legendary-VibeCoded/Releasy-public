Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Releasy runs on your own computer. Your tracks, cover art, lyrics and projects stay there. This page explains the little data we do handle, and why.

Who is responsible. Legendary Tunes, a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KVK) under number 74813455, based in IJsselstein, the Netherlands. Email: support@legendaryhq.net. More company details are under "Legal details" at the bottom of the page.

The short version

  • Releasy has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. We do not sell data.
  • Your audio, images, videos and lyrics are never uploaded anywhere. They stay on your computer.
  • We only keep what we need to deliver your license, activate it and help you if something goes wrong.

What we handle, and why

When you buy. Payments are handled by Paddle, our merchant of record. Paddle collects your payment details and handles tax. We receive the information needed to deliver your license and give support: your email address, your country and your order number. Paddle has its own privacy policy for the data it collects. Why: to carry out the purchase (contract) and to meet bookkeeping and tax duties (legal obligation).

Your license key. When you enter your key, Releasy sends the key, a random computer ID that the app created for itself, and the app version to our activation server. The computer ID is not based on your hardware, name or email. We store the key, the computer ID and the activation date, so that one key works on one computer. The server runs on Cloudflare, which processes your IP address while handling the request. Why: to run the license (contract) and to prevent misuse (legitimate interest).

Emails and support. We email your key to the address you bought with, using an email service that works on our behalf. If you contact us, we keep your message and our reply so we can help you. Why: contract and legitimate interest.

The free trial. The date your trial started is stored only on your computer. We do not receive it.

This website. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses. The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads. The site uses no cookies and no analytics. The Releasy app can also fetch fonts from Google Fonts when you choose a font.

Feedback form. If you use our feedback form (run by Google Forms), Google processes what you submit, and we read it to fix problems.

Who else sees data

Only the services above: Paddle (payment and tax), Cloudflare (the activation server), GitHub (website hosting), Google (fonts and the feedback form) and our email service. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it. Some of these services are based outside the European Union. Where they transfer personal data, they rely on approved safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Order and invoice records: as long as the law requires, normally 7 years.
  • License key, computer ID and activation date: while your license is in use, and for as long afterwards as is needed to handle support or disputes.
  • Support emails: until the matter is settled, and not much longer.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive it in a portable format. Email us at support@legendaryhq.net and we will answer within one month. Some data we must keep by law, for example invoices. If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Security

Because your files stay on your computer, they are as safe as your computer is. For the data we do hold, we use reputable services and keep access to ourselves.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version appears here with a new date. If a change matters to you, we will say so on the website.
