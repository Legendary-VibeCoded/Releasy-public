Contact

Last updated 26 November 2026

Questions, a lost key, or a problem? Here is how to reach me.

Email: support@legendaryhq.net

Developer: Legendary Tunes

Lost or moved your key

Email me from the address you bought with, and include your order number if you have it. I can resend your key, or reset it so you can activate it on a new computer. You can also do this yourself: in Releasy, open the license window, enter your key and choose Move license to this computer with the email address you bought with.

Found a bug or have an idea

Use the feedback form. In Releasy, open Settings → About and press Copy version info, then paste it into the form. It helps me find the problem faster.
